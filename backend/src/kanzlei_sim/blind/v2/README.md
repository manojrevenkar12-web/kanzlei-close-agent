# Blind set v2

Two invented tax firms with three clients each. The month is September 2026. Every client has a
history of up to twelve months for its regular partners. The set was written from
`../WRITING_CASES.md` alone, without looking at the system's code, configuration, reports or other sets.

## Firm A: Roth & Weiland Steuerberatungsgesellschaft mbH, Kassel (SKR03)

**Elektro Hainbach GmbH** (rw-2101, OPOS, Soll, monthly): an electrical contractor with one
managing director, two electricians and an apprentice. Its month:
- material invoices from its regular wholesaler, one paid less a cash discount and two paid in one
  transfer;
- cable bought from a Dutch supplier, and a German subcontractor who lays cable on a new-build site;
- a large invoice to a general contractor for that site, smaller jobs for a housing cooperative
  (two invoices settled in one payment), a private wallbox installation and a doctor's practice;
- a new garage's invoice for one of the vans;
- an installation tester ordered online;
- an Innung contribution, a DGUV seminar and a business dinner;
- card purchases at a DIY store and a tool shop;
- an unexplained credit from a country inn after an emergency call-out;
- the usual rent, energy, phone, leasing, insurance, payroll, social security, wage tax and VAT
  prepayment.

**Niklas Brandauer Softwareentwicklung** (rw-2102, direct, Ist, quarterly): a sole-trader DevOps
freelancer working from a coworking desk. His month:
- customers in Austria and Germany, a new small customer, and a payment for an older invoice that
  came without explanation;
- cloud, SaaS and licence charges from the US, Ireland and the Czech Republic, an online course
  from the US, and German hosting;
- a conference trip to Berlin (ticket, train, hotel, taxi);
- a client lunch, a new notebook, an external SSD, business cards and parking;
- an invoice from a graphic designer;
- toner ordered online, and a toy bought on the business card;
- private health insurance, a gym, an income-tax prepayment and owner's withdrawals.

**Ilona Marek Übersetzungen** (rw-2103, direct, Kleinunternehmerin): a Polish–German translator and
court interpreter working from home. Her month:
- payments from a translation agency, a Polish law firm, the local court (JVEG) and two private
  customers for certified translations;
- a subcontracted job to a colleague;
- CAT tool, online dictionary, phone and a small credit from the phone company;
- association dues, a webinar, a regional train ticket and a monitor;
- stamps and stationery from the same shop, a copy-shop receipt, a coffee with a lawyer and a
  drugstore purchase;
- a transfer from a relative in Poland;
- health insurance, an income-tax prepayment, a card fee and her withdrawals.

## Firm B: Albers Hoffmann Steuerberater PartG mbB, Osnabrück (SKR04)

**Tafelwerk Catering GmbH** (ah-3101, OPOS, Soll, monthly): a caterer for company lunches and
events, with a managing director, a cook, a kitchen help and a driver. Its month:
- food wholesale invoices with mixed rates, collected less a cash discount;
- a bakery, and a vegetable farm (two invoices paid together);
- temp service staff, compostable boxes from the Netherlands and a new combi steamer;
- a knife grinder's invoice;
- weekly lunch invoices to an engineering company, a law-firm reception (paid less a cash
  discount) and a club's summer party;
- a credit from the landlord;
- US and Irish software and advertising charges;
- a dinner with a prospective customer;
- card purchases at a cash-and-carry and a farm shop;
- the usual rent, energy, phone, van leasing, payroll and taxes.

**Logopädische Praxis Henrike Janssen** (ah-3102, direct, tax-exempt, quarterly): a speech therapy
practice with one employed therapist and a mini-jobber at reception. Its month:
- payments from three statutory health insurers and two self-paying patients;
- therapy material from a publisher and a toy shop;
- a tablet, practice software and the telematics fee;
- a training course, association dues and a lunch with a paediatrician;
- toner, a pharmacy purchase and a coat bought on the card;
- a transfer from the owner's husband;
- payroll, social security (including the mini-job office), wage tax, an income-tax prepayment
  and withdrawals.

**Zweirad Overbeck e.K.** (ah-3103, direct, Soll, monthly): a bike shop with a workshop, a
mechanic and an apprentice. Its month:
- e-bikes and parts from German distributors and a Dutch wholesaler;
- an invoice from a one-man wheel builder, and brake parts ordered online;
- weekly card-terminal payouts and the acquirer's fee;
- cash deposits from the till, a bike sold to a company-bike leasing firm, and a customer's
  unexplained transfer;
- a trip to a dealer fair in Frankfurt (train, hotel, a dinner with a supplier's rep);
- newspaper and Irish online ads, a new repair stand and torque wrenches, and a van service;
- garden furniture bought on the card;
- the usual rent, energy, phone, till software, insurance, payroll, VAT prepayment, an income-tax
  prepayment and withdrawals.

Each client has some items the firm will want to ask about or look at: missing or incomplete
receipts, private purchases, unexplained credits, meals without details, foreign suppliers and
larger asset purchases. The client's answers are in the files.

# 3. GoBD: unchangeable after sign-off, documents in their original form

Status: accepted

## Context
The GoBD require that bookings, once final, cannot be changed without a trace, that documents are kept
in the form they arrived in (an XRechnung is the XML, not a rendering of it), and that the procedure is
documented. Since BEG IV, Buchungsbelege are kept for 8 years.

## Decision
- **Festschreibung:** `sign_off` writes the VAT return and the export checksum and sets the close to
  `festgeschrieben`. Triggers (`forbid_locked_booking`, `forbid_closed_booking`, `forbid_closed_item`)
  refuse any change to bookings or items of that month, for every role. Corrections go into an open
  month. The DATEV export carries the Festschreibung flag.
- **Protocol:** every decision is an event in an append-only table (no UPDATE/DELETE for the app role)
  whose rows are hash-chained; `verify_events()` and `kanzlei verify-audit` check the chain.
- **Documents:** stored once under their SHA-256 with `IfNoneMatch`, never overwritten. In production
  the bucket has S3 Object Lock in compliance mode with an 8-year default retention
  (`deploy/terraform/storage.tf`), so not even an administrator can delete a document early.

## Consequences
- Re-running the agent on a signed month is refused, not silently ignored.
- Records needing 10 years (Bücher, Jahresabschlüsse) live in DATEV, which stays the books of record;
  this system's exports are the trail to them.
